\title{ERA5-Conditioned Block Imputation for Sparse Antarctic Weather Station Time Series}

\author{Anonymous Authors}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
Antarctic automatic weather stations provide essential in situ meteorological observations, but their time series are often sparse because of sensor icing, power interruptions, and winter maintenance gaps. We study block-wise imputation for 3-hourly Antarctic station records using ERA5 as an external physical prior. We construct an AntAWS-derived benchmark with 32 selected stations, aligned ERA5 time series, realistic block-missing simulation, and held-out station splits, and use a lightweight ERA5-conditioned variate-token transformer to test when reanalysis information is useful. Removing ERA5 degrades block-imputation MAE by 34\%, while gated and concat fusion perform similarly, indicating that the information source and missingness regime matter more than a specific fusion layer. Follow-up analyses show that the ERA5 benefit grows from 13.8\% for 24-hour blocks to 26.4\% for 216-hour blocks, is concentrated in temperature, wind speed, and humidity channels, and remains stable when ERA5 is downsampled to 6-hour resolution.
\end{abstract}
